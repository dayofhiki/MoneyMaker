## Opportunity versus policy and execution truth

Among execution-day events,10/20 have whole-path net5 opportunity, but only4/20
retain it before the fixed stop. Six can rise later only after the fixed entry
has already stopped. A9:35 entry plus simple terminal/stop hold yields mean
BASE return **-1.73%** for execution-day events (controls-1.42%). Thus the
upside-frequency signal is not evidence that buying every split day makes money.
The exact-8K simple terminal mean is-1.25% (controls-1.45%).

Sparse prints matter:18/20 execution-day events and56/60 controls have a>=60s
interprint gap. Entry reference delays>=60s occur in8/20 events and42/60
controls. Exit reference delays>=60s occur in8/20 and34/60. Maximum terminal
pending gaps reach1523s for events and12497s for controls. Across the complete
436-row sample,387 rows have a recorded>=60s gap.

These next-print references are scenario labels. They do not establish NBBO
availability, size, market impact or official halt status. Controls are often
sparser than event stocks despite previous-day matching, so the observed tail
contrast also reflects liquidity/observation differences. Do not claim these
ceilings are executable returns without an explicit execution validation.

## Research decision

No primary family passes the fixed support/material gate. Do not change the
main momentum model or add event-conditioned experts/router from R316B.
The independent event data/clock pipeline is now usable; the two strongest
next needs are repeated structural-event support and executable entry timing.

A next experiment must be separately preregistered rather than enlarge or tune
R316B in response to these results. Replicate execution-day primary and all
fixed secondary windows with enough independent issuer/date clusters; preserve
all census and missingness ledgers and compare with controls. Keep the same
risk/cost definitions and explicitly audit announcement linkage and sparse-print
execution. Only after a repeatable event-specific distribution and simple
context/interaction test should specialist routing be considered. June HOLD
and July-August stay closed, and reused May remains development.
