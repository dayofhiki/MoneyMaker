# Request 154 — Active admission score-family decomposition

## Motivation

Request 153 localized the remaining handoff loss. Of 1,114 runner crossings on
the already-opened May 21-28 block, 684 had a causal exact prior pre-runner
state and 669 of those were already inside Focus-60 (97.8%). The main residual
loss is therefore inside Focus-60 -> Active-20 selection.

The direct unconditional model improved full-refresh retention only from 92.83%
to 93.27% (+0.45pp) and reduced Focus average precision from 0.1265 to 0.1228.
It failed the preregistered 1pp improvement threshold.

## Question

Before adding new features, is the weakness caused by the *structure* of the
admission score, especially the observability multiplier or the population on
which the admission model is trained?

## Frozen structural variants

Focus-60 membership remains generated by the existing market-hazard model.
Transport survival, capacity 20 and allocator mechanics remain unchanged.

Compare four admission scores:

1. **legacy**: market hazard × next-minute observability;
2. **hazard_only**: market hazard alone, removing the observability penalty;
3. **global_direct**: request-153 direct unconditional one-minute crossing model;
4. **focus_direct**: the same direct one-minute target, but fit only on historical
   rows that the frozen Focus-60 selector would admit.

Each score is also multiplied by the same transport-survival probability for
stale-incumbent eviction value. Evaluate max additions 8 and 20.

## Preregistered interpretation

The primary diagnostic is cap=20 retention versus legacy.

- **structural_candidate_found**: a nonlegacy variant reaches >=95% and improves
  on legacy by >=1 percentage point.
- **partial_structural_gain**: the best variant gains >=1 point but remains below
  95%.
- **feature_information_bottleneck**: no structural variant gains 1 point.

Report Focus average precision and per-day/cap=8 metrics as secondary evidence.
They do not rewrite the primary diagnosis.

If a structural candidate is found, run an integrated same-block HOT check
before any fresh validation. If no candidate is found, the next experiment must
enrich causal admission information rather than tune transport capacity.
