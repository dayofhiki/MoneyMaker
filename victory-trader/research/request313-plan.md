# R313 — information arrival versus the cost of waiting

Registered after freezing local R312 failures and before selecting/scoring/labeling later observations. Reuse only original R311828 May identities and raw-second checkpoint. No new fits beyond reproducing frozen R311 M, no new features, no acquisition or sealed dates. This is reused development; no official run yet and no policy promotion.

"Print" means a recorded one-second aggregate bar, not an individual execution. Two-print evidence means two distinct traded seconds; a single bar may contain multiple transactions. The study does not reconstruct tick-level tape or quotes.

Original observation is unchanged R311 first completed regular print starting at/after HOT and before HOT+300s. Evidence-arrival observation is the FIRST such print whose strictly completed last10s window contains at least two prints. Counts use bar-completion timestamps in (decision-10s,decision]; observation must also precede the unchanged min(HOT+60min,session-close) cap. It may equal the original observation. Selection reads timestamps only, before any labels; no scan for favorable returns, future fill completeness, high scores, alternative window/print counts or best delay. No forward-fill/interpolation. Missing evidence remains unavailable, even for an original winner.

Fit the unchanged28-input M model once on official original R3104,526 May5–8 states using exact R311 seed20264605/C=.1/transform/weights. Original scores must replay1e-9. Recompute original clock inputs and labels for integrity. Rebuild unchanged clock/background/cost inputs at evidence arrival, with original completed-minute HOT context and decision-time costs. Apply identical net5 label rules, BASE costs/-2.5% absorbing stop/pending next-print fills/common HOT+60min-session cap. Persist all828 rows, original/evidence clocks, missing reasons, features/scores/labels and input hashes.

Primary descriptive endpoint: evidence-minus-original same-day weighted AUROC on identities with BOTH complete labels and available observations, paired ticker-day/day bootstrap1000 draws seed20264850+cluster. Labels now reflect different entry times, so this is an observation/target-timing diagnostic, NOT pure model increment. Report pooled AUC/AP/Brier, same-clock/changed-clock counts, original/evidence positive2x2 table, originally positive cases with unavailable/incomplete later observation, additional wait quantiles and missing reasons. A rank improvement cannot override discarded opportunities or unseen identities.

No research promotion gate or data-driven wait policy is constructed. This study cannot assess continuous controller PnL, bid/ask fills, or whether a future dynamic wait decision can retain an individual missed surge. It measures whether the fixed original window/count contract creates informative evidence at an acceptable opportunity cost. Findings must preserve matched-population selection, missing fills, reused dates and target-timing limits.
