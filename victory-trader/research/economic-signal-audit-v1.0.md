# Request 164 — economic signal audit and fixed-horizon bridge

Request 164 opens no new dates and changes no model, threshold, feature set,
cost assumption, or Request-163 selector.

It reuses Request-163 prepared artifacts to answer three diagnostic questions:

1. Why were only about 57-68% of fresh first-HOT events economically labelable?
2. Which already-causal ENTRY_FEATURES are associated with positive economic
   opportunity in development history, and do those directions remain
   consistent on June 8-12?
3. Under the already-frozen Request-163 opportunity selector, what happens if
   we replace the hindsight "best exit within 30 minutes" evaluation with
   mechanical fixed exits at 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, and 30 minutes using the existing
   BASE round-trip cost model?

The June results are diagnostic only. No feature or exit horizon will be chosen
or tuned from these results and then claimed as fresh evidence.

A positive fixed-horizon mean is stronger evidence toward executable economics
than the oracle target, but is still not portfolio profitability because it
does not yet model overlapping positions, capital limits, position sizing, or
a recurrent HOLD/EXIT policy.
