# R331 proposal: action-target observability before another value model

Not executed or formally registered. This proposal follows the fixed R330 failure,
not a relabeling of R330. Freeze final source/input hashes and the complete census
contract remotely before creating any new late-phase clocks/labels or fitting.

## Evidence and choice

R329 has a limited robust whole-episode smoothing increment, but fails local,
admission and probability-error criteria. Explicit memory is low priority.
R330 implements recurrent current-state action decisions and a separate capital
replay, but passes only8/18 gates. F and E both choose cash on every clock:
training Q_ENTER range[−2.129167,−.611352]%, evaluation[−2.648053,−.571570]%.
WAIT comparison cannot be credited or blamed when the shared ENTER head never
identifies positive EV.69 evaluation episodes have at least one observed positive
fixed60s BASE outcome, so blanket cash is not a successful aggressive trader.

Economic-target support is independently inadequate. Only104 training episodes
have a common ENTER/WAIT target versus the preregistered150 floor. Even removing
WAIT target availability would yield only114 ENTER-labeled episodes, so an
uncoupled loss alone does not fix that shortage. The original observation-weight
mass is266, common targets carry34.640426 and all immediate targets38.638358:
dense rows conceal little independent/effectively weighted action support.

G and G0 choose the same330 episode entries, with215 late/missing entry references
and56 late/missing exits; only59 resolve (BASE mean−1.181321% on those59 only).
Its2-slot account reaches two unknown reserved orders on every date; full account
returns are unknown. R245 already showed learnable execution availability does
not itself create profitability. Do not just repeat a tradability classifier or
interpret avoiding unresolved losses as positive trading skill.

**Next priority is a cached action-target support census, not another model.**
Determine whether the inherited5-minute watch truncation prevents observing
sufficient causal, economically labeled entry opportunities. The fixed60-minute
HOT/session cap already exists in the raw caches. Testing its later observation
phases is a separately registered population/time intervention, not added model
memory and not a search for a profitable holding duration.

## Fixed census design

Use exactly the original407 R315 broad training identities (keep May5–8 and
May6–8 counts separately) and828 R311/R319 evaluation identities. Authenticate
existing cohort/raw archives; no new market requests, dates or event memberships.
Keep all unobserved, unavailable and censored identities in ledgers. These are
reused development cohorts. There is no fresh validation or promotion.

Early arm: inherited original completed-print observation clocks through HOT+300s.
Extended arm: retain the early clocks and add the FIRST completed regular print
in each fixed30s bucket after300s until min(HOT+60min,session close). Buckets are
anchored to HOT; the first print is chosen by timestamps as it completes, not
by a later high/low, dense interval or profitable outcome. A bucket without a
print creates no observation; fixed watch timers still expire. Save a clock
manifest and source identities before any price/outcome construction.

Use the exact R330 ENTER downstream contract: next-print entry/exit<=3s, completed-
close BASE−2.5% absorbing stop, fixed60s hold capped by original HOT/session terminal,
unchanged LIGHT/BASE/STRESS costs. Do not change the holding period, stop or lag
bound to get more resolved labels. Unknown executions stay unknown. No best
future exit or maximum-return target. Past120s current features and known cost
may be replayed for a future learner, with prefix/future invariance tests; no
current/late outcome enters them. Old early-window G transport is not assumed
valid in the new phase, and no late policy head is fitted in this census.

Report EARLY, ADDITIONAL_LATE and EXTENDED union without selecting the best phase:
observed clocks/identities, immediate target resolution, complete independent
episodes, original observation-weight mass, positive/negative BASE target episode
support by date, causal cost-compatible support (known cost<2.5%), fill delays,
stop/cost-only breaches and cost-scenario payoff distributions. Keep a paired
identity ledger showing gained/lost/unchanged support. More seconds are not more
independent examples. Full unknown means and conditional known means remain distinct.

## Decision gates and follow-on policy

Main feasibility scope is May6–8, matching the strict-past G/value training scope.
Require>=150 independent cost-compatible complete ENTER target episodes and>=30
positive BASE target episodes across all3 dates, with both signs on all3 dates.
Save the same evaluation support metrics, but never choose clocks/thresholds from
evaluation profitability. Use day/ticker-day clustered support intervals and
retain every failed requirement. Predetermine a90% resolution target for an
execution-conditioned policy dataset; failure must remain visible.

If support improves sufficiently, preregister a new current-state action-value
experiment on ALL declared phases with explicitly rebuilt chronological quality/
execution/value components. Separate immediate payoff, fill/continuation support,
WAIT continuation under a frozen causal baseline and downside. A small fixed
nonlinear or payoff-decomposed model is only justified after this support audit;
no optimizer/feature/threshold sweep is authorized by a census improvement.

If the fixed cache/contract remains too sparse, prioritize execution-observation
data or a separately justified order/fill contract rather than assume missing
values are losses/cash or invent new memory. Quotes/size/actual receipts would
need their own explicit source and collection design; next-print proxies do not
become executable simply because a model predicts them. Do not open sealed dates
or the event track to rescue support. R316/R316B remains independent.

After sufficient absolute economic value is learned, the next policy questions
remain learned HOLD/EXIT, capital opportunity cost versus other tickers, and
allocation/ADD/REDUCE. R330's ledger engine can support those later experiments,
but its current all-cash result is not a promoted trader.
